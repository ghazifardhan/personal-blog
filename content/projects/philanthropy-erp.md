# Philanthropy ERP System

This ERP platform was designed and built to modernize the digital infrastructure of non-profit organizations. It centralizes fragmented operations into a single, high-performance system.

## 🚀 Key Impact

- **30%+ Donation Growth**: Optimized the donation pipeline and user experience, leading to a significant measurable increase in donor conversion.
- **50% Operational Efficiency**: Automated manual processes in Program & Donation management, reducing administrative overhead by half.
- **Scalable Architecture**: Built to handle 10K+ concurrent users during peak donation seasons (Ramadan/Qurban).

## 🛠 Core Modules

- **Program & Donations Management**: Real-time tracking of philanthropic programs and transparent reporting for donors.
- **CRM & Affiliate System**: Managing donor relationships and empowering affiliate marketers to drive growth.
- **Finance & Accounting**: Automated ledger systems with a closed-loop wallet for internal transactions.
- **Workflow Automation**: Integrated **n8n** to handle complex asynchronous tasks and automated communications.

## ⚙️ Technical Highlights

- **Backend**: High-concurrency Golang microservices.
- **Frontend**: Responsive React.js dashboard for administrators and stakeholders.
- **Mobile Companion**: Flutter-based apps for donors and field agents.
- **DevOps**: Automated CI/CD pipelines with comprehensive automated testing.
