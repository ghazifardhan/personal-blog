AI is making content cheaper. That means personal websites are becoming more important, not less.

When anyone can generate a decent article, landing page, logo, or code snippet in seconds, the scarce thing is no longer production. The scarce thing is identity, taste, trust, and continuity.

A personal website gives you all four.

---

## Social Platforms Are Rented Land

Posting on social media is useful, but it is not ownership.

Algorithms change. Accounts get suspended. Links rot. Platforms rise and fall. Your work becomes trapped inside someone else's feed.

A personal website is different. It is your domain, your archive, your design, your rules.

That matters more in an AI era where feeds are increasingly filled with synthetic noise.

---

## Your Website Becomes Proof of Work

A good personal site shows a timeline of what you actually build, think, and care about.

It can include:

- Blog posts
- Project notes
- Tools
- Experiments
- Talks
- Case studies
- Reading lists
- Open-source work

This creates a public memory that is difficult to fake overnight.

AI can generate a single article. It cannot easily fake years of consistent curiosity.

---

## Taste Becomes the Differentiator

When everyone has access to the same AI tools, output volume matters less. Taste matters more.

Your website shows taste through:

- What you choose to write about
- Which projects you build
- How you explain things
- What you leave out
- How the site feels
- Whether the details are polished

AI can help you produce, but taste decides what is worth producing.

---

## Personal Sites Can Become AI Interfaces

The next version of a personal website might not only be pages. It could be an interface to your work.

Imagine visitors asking:

- What projects has this person built with Nuxt?
- Summarize their AI articles.
- Which tools on this site help with images?
- What is their deployment stack?

A structured personal website with content, metadata, and APIs can become readable by both humans and agents.

That is powerful.

---

## What to Build Now

If you are starting today, keep it simple:

- Own a domain
- Write useful articles
- Publish real projects
- Add small tools
- Use structured metadata
- Keep your content in Git
- Make the site fast

Do not wait for the perfect design. A living website beats a perfect Notion draft every time.

---

## Final Thoughts

AI will flood the web with average content. That makes authentic, long-term personal spaces more valuable.

Your website is not just a portfolio. It is your public memory, your proof of work, and your home base on the internet.

In the AI era, owning that home base is a serious advantage.
